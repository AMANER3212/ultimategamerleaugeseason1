Official Rules & Regulations

Mandatory guidelines for fair play, player conduct, and event integrity.

Fair Play & Conduct

  • **Zero Tolerance for Cheating:** Use of hacks, scripts, third-party software, or any unauthorized modification is strictly prohibited and results in immediate permanent ban.
  • **Account Sharing:** Sharing accounts or playing on someone's account is not allowed.
  • **Toxic Behavior:** Any form of verbal abuse, harassment, or offensive communication toward opponents, teammates, or administrators will lead to disqualification.
  • **Referees' Decision:** All decisions made by the tournament administrators and referees are final and binding.

Eligibility & Registration

  • **Eligibility:** The tournament is open to all students. ID verification is spot-checked by admins.
  • **Registration & Fees:** Registration is confirmed only upon successful submission of the form details and payment of the specified entry fee (₹50/₹100/₹200).
  • **Mode:** Select between **Online (Remote)** and **Offline (On-Site/LAN)** when registering. Detailed instructions for your chosen mode will be provided after payment confirmation.
  • **Lateness:** Teams/Players must be ready at the designated match time. Late arrival (beyond 10 minutes) may result in automatic forfeit.
  • **Disclaimers:** The organizers reserve the right to change the schedule, rules, or prizes if necessary, with prior notice.
